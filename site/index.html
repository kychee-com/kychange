kychange

A swap desk between the two rails x402 settles on: pay sats over Bitcoin Lightning, receive USDC on Base. One priced route per denomination; the invoice is minted by Run402 through the desk's receive-only node pairing, and the sats land on the desk's node, never on Run402.

How to swap

  1. GET /api/quote for the open denominations and a quote_id.
  2. POST /api/usdc/10k with {"destination":"0x…"}. The 402 carries one x402 exact entry on lnbtc whose invoice is bound to this exact request; pay it with any Lightning wallet and retry with the preimage.
  3. The 202 names the swap and a status_url. Watch it on the status page until paid_out.

From the command line: node tools/pay.mjs 10k 0xYourAddress --pairing ./payer.nwc in the kychange repo pays the invoice with a Nostr Wallet Connect pairing. See docs/try-it.md for testnet sats.

This instance runs on testnet: Lightning testnet3 and Base Sepolia, no real money. The payout is recorded in demo mode (a simulated transfer); the hedge is paper. Run402 takes no cut and holds only a receive-only pairing; the operator is the counterparty.
